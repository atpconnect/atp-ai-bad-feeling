Section 03
Ground Control: Getting In
The Alive in Roswell street festival runs the same evening in downtown Roswell. Expect heavy foot traffic and street closures around Canton Street.
Recommended approach
Take an Uber or Lyft.
It skips the parking hunt entirely, and you won't be stranded with a car downtown when we walk to the festival at 8:30. Set the drop-off to 1098 Green St and ask for the Green Street entrance — if Canton Street is closed, your driver will route around it. For the ride home, walk a block off Canton before requesting; surge and pickup chaos are worst right at the festival.
If you're driving
01 Gravel lot — east side of the venue
The small gravel lot directly beside Social House, off Green Street. Shortest walk of any option — you park and you're at the door. It holds very few cars and it is the first thing to fill, so only count on it if you're arriving before 6:00.
1098 Green St, Roswell, GA 30075 ›
02 Parking garage — 1056 Alpharetta Street
The public deck northeast of the venue. Use the entrance nearest Green Street, off Kevin Cash Memorial Drive, then walk back down Green Street to Social House — about three or four minutes on flat sidewalk. Most capacity of anything close by, but the festival crowd will take it fast; park on an upper level rather than circling the lower ones.
1056 Alpharetta St, Roswell, GA 30075 ›
03 Soccer complex lot — west side of Green Street
Across Green Street from the venue, past the coffee houses. Usually the quietest option on a weeknight and a five-minute walk. Cross at the marked crosswalk rather than mid-block — Green Street traffic gets festival-heavy after 6:00.
Green Street, Roswell, GA 30075 ›
04 Free parking + free trolley — Roswell City Hall
The reliable play if you're arriving close to 6:00. Park free in the City Hall lot, then catch the free festival trolley to the Historic Town Square and walk a few blocks to Social House. The trolley runs continuously through the evening, so it's also an easy ride back to your car at the end of the night.
38 Hill St, Roswell, GA 30075 ›
05 Free parking — Woodstock Park
The second free lot, on the Woodstock Road side of downtown. Same idea as City Hall: leave the car, then trolley or walk in. Good backup if City Hall is already full when you get there.
Woodstock Park, Roswell, GA ›
Whichever you choose: leave 20–30 minutes earlier than you think you need to, and expect to walk a few blocks in whatever shoes you pick. If you end up circling, the City Hall lot plus the trolley is faster than waiting for a space downtown.
