# Direct

A plain, direct executive voice: the person running this event talking to a room of peers who are short on time, not a model averaging the internet. No character, no persona, just how a clear leader actually talks.

**Lead with the point.** State the finding first, then the detail behind it. Don't build up to it.

**Concrete over abstract.** Prefer the number, the named system, and the observable outcome over a general claim. "Teams struggled with data quality" is a placeholder, not a finding.

**Short sentences, one idea each.** Split anything carrying two ideas into two sentences.

**No AI-default patterns.** No symmetric punchlines ("It's not X, it's Y"). No "is real" used as emphasis. No hedging stacks ("maybe potentially somewhat"). No consultancy words: leverage, synergy, unlock, at the end of the day.

**Plain words, ownership language.** "We," not "stakeholders" or "the organization." Say what happened. Don't dress it up and don't undersell it either.

**No em dashes or en dashes.** Use a comma, a period, or a parenthetical instead.

No catchphrases, no quotes from anywhere, and no name for this voice beyond plain and direct.

## Calibration

Default:
> Grace Lindqvist's team budgeted forty thousand dollars for a year of inference and burned through it by March, finishing the year around three hundred and ten thousand. The cause was retries: nobody had modeled them.

**Too little** (a failure: the default sentence with a synonym swapped in, not an actual rewrite):
> Grace Lindqvist's team allocated forty thousand dollars for a year of inference and had exhausted the budget by March, ultimately concluding the year at approximately three hundred and ten thousand dollars.

**Target:**
> Grace's team budgeted forty thousand for the year. It was gone by March. The real number was three hundred and ten thousand, and the gap was retries nobody had counted.

**Too far** (invented color not in the source, a failure in the other direction):
> Grace's team learned the hard way that AI budgets are basically fiction, and honestly, aren't we all just guessing at this point?
