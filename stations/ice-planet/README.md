## Station pilot

<img src="../../assets/people/mat-mathews.jpg" alt="Mat Mathews" width="160" align="right">

**Mat Mathews**, Chief Information Officer, Fortrex.

Mat leads Fortrex's digital and AI programs with a focus on measurable operational and cost efficiency. His work spans corporate IT and the OT environments across Fortrex's sanitation operations and its Safe Foods chemical manufacturing plants.

He has carried both the CIO and CISO roles, leading infrastructure, data, and AI initiatives and answering for both the business case and the risk posture. Before joining Fortrex, he was Chief Technology Officer at Boys & Girls Clubs of America. Earlier in his career, he worked in the U.S. defense and intelligence community, delivering systems under federal security accreditation and classified data-handling requirements.
