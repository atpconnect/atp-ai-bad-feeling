## Station pilot

<img src="../../assets/people/dante-jackson.jpg" alt="Dante Jackson" width="160" align="right">

**Dante Jackson**, Founder and CEO, Serket-Tech Security.

Dante founded Serket-Tech Security, a cybersecurity consulting firm based in Atlanta. He brings more than two decades of experience in information security, insider threat investigations, and data protection across the financial services and federal government sectors.

Before founding Serket-Tech, he held a succession of senior cybersecurity leadership roles at Truist, including Head of Security Resilience, Director of Insider Threat, Business Information Security Manager, and Cybersecurity Data Protection Manager, where he built enterprise-wide insider threat and data loss prevention programs, led risk assessments, and reduced security incidents while safeguarding sensitive financial data. Earlier in his career, he served as a Special Agent with the Department of Justice, directing complex international investigations into organized crime, financial fraud, and domestic terrorism, and leading a 15-person Safe Streets Gang Task Force.

Dante holds an MBA in Management of Technology from the Georgia Institute of Technology, and a Master of Science and Bachelor of Arts in Criminal Justice from Sam Houston State University. He is an Executive Board Member of Inspiredu.
